Detailed Description

The thermal system is comprised of various thermal components. Heat is transferred to these components, and a basic caliorimetric calculation is performed to determine the change in temperature of the system. This is a very simple representation, in that it is one dimensional and time-independent, So we are not worried about time-dependent heat transfer, only the transfer itself (we assume the transfer "works" on the timescales in which we are interested).
