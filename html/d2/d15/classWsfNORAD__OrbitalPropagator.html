Detailed Description

WsfNORAD_OrbitalPropagator is a specialization of WsfMover for earth-orbiting satellites.

This mover incorporates the SG* and SD* propagators for "near-earth" (orbits > 6.4 revs / day) and "deep-space" (orbits < 6.4 revs / day) satellites. These are published in the "SpaceTrack 3" report: http://celestrak.com/NORAD/documentation/spacetrk.pdf. They take into account perturbation effects from the sun and moon. This code is based upon a public domain c-based adaptation of the algorithms. This adaptation can be found at http://www.projectpluto.com/sat_code.htm .
