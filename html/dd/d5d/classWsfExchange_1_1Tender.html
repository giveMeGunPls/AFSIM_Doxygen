Detailed Description

Tender, or offer of goods or services. Represents the amount and rate of an exchanged good or service. Services are exchanged in non-dimensional values (i.e. repairs per second), while goods may be exchanged either non-dimensionally, or in mass-rate (kg/hr. etc.).
